PlayGoo privacy policy

Effective from {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

In short

  • We collect no personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Drawings, stars, stickers and settings stay on the device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, set up for children: no personalised ads, no advertising ID, family-friendly content only.
  • "Remove ads" is a one-time purchase through Google Play. We never see payment details.
  • The app does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or files, and has no analytics or tracking of its own.

1. Who we are

PlayGoo is published by {{PUBLISHER}}. For anything about privacy, write to {{EMAIL}}.

2. Information kept only on the device

The app saves the following in its own storage on the device:

  • progress: the stars and stickers earned, and the sticker pictures made,
  • settings: sound on or off, difficulty levels, small or capital letters and similar choices,
  • whether "Remove ads" has been bought, so ads stay off without a network connection.

None of this is sent anywhere, and we cannot see it. Letters and patterns drawn with a finger are checked on the device and are neither saved nor sent. Sounds are made by the device.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the menu screens, and now and then a full-screen ad between activities, never during one. Every ad request is marked as coming from an app for children (under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and as users under the age of consent in Europe), which means:

  • ads are never personalised and no interest-based or remarketing ads are shown,
  • the app does not read or send the device's advertising ID,
  • only ads rated suitable for general audiences, including families, are shown,
  • only ad networks certified under Google Play's Families policy are used.

To deliver an ad and protect against fraud, Google receives technical information such as the IP address, device model, operating system version, language and which ads were shown or tapped. Google handles it under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive this information. Buying "Remove ads" stops all ad requests.

4. The "Remove ads" purchase

"Remove ads" is bought through Google Play, behind a question for grown-ups. Google handles the payment under the Google Play terms and its privacy policy. The app only learns whether the purchase has been made, so it can switch the ads off; we receive no name, email address or payment details.

5. Children

PlayGoo is made for young children learning to write. It asks for no information, has no chat, accounts or sharing, and keeps purchases and the link to this page behind a question for grown-ups. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Google Play

The app is installed from Google Play, and Google processes information about installs and updates under its own privacy policy. Apart from the ads and the purchase described above, the app sends Google nothing.

7. Deleting information

Everything the app keeps is on the device. "Clear progress" under "For grown-ups" deletes the stars and stickers; uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything. A purchase of "Remove ads" stays with the Google account and can be restored on any device.

8. Your rights

If you are in the UK or the European Union you have rights over personal data, including access, correction and deletion. As we hold no personal data, there is usually nothing for us to give or delete, but you are welcome to ask at the address in section 1. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

9. Changes

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
